# Administrator Guide

- Change the seeded administrator password immediately.
- Maintain company GST/PAN/address/bank details under Company Profile.
- Configure branches, default warehouses, financial years and numbering before transactions.
- Close or lock financial years only after reconciliation and backup.
- Use separate thermal, A4 and label printer mappings.
- Configure SMTP/SMS secrets through protected configuration; never expose credentials in screenshots or exports.
- Review failed logins, audit events, low stock, outstanding balances and backup results daily.
- Create verified full backups before releases and financial-year closure. Test recovery regularly on a separate database/server.
- Grant users only the permissions required for their role. Review administrator membership monthly.
